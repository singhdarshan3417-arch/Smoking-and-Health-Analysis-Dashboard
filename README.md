# Smoking-and-Health-Analysis-Dashboard

## project Objective
The main purpose is to analyze patients' smoking habits, health conditions, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and demographics to help management understand overall patient health patterns.

## Data Used
-<a href="https://github.com/singhdarshan3417-arch/Smoking-and-Health-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/Smoking_Health_Analysis.csv.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions
Calculate the total number of patients.
Calculate the total number of current smokers.
Calculate the total number of patients with a recorded organ category.
Calculate the total number of patients with a recorded health condition.
Age Group: Create age categories to group patients into different age ranges, such as 18–29, 30–44, 45–59, and 60+.
BP Risk Category: Create categories based on systolic and diastolic blood pressure readings, such as Lower Range, Elevated, and High.
Analyze average cigarettes consumed per day by smoking status.
Analyze organ-wise health condition distribution.
Analyze smoking status distribution among patients.
Compare average cholesterol levels across blood pressure risk categories.
Analyze the number of current smokers across different age groups.
Analyze the distribution of recorded health conditions across different levels of estimated smoking exposure.

## Process
Import and understand the dataset


Clean and transform data in Power Query


Create data relationships and verify columns


Create calculated columns using DAX


Create KPI measures


Build charts for all four objectives


Design and format the dashboard


Analyze insights and prepare the conclusion

## Project Insights

The Smoking and Health Analysis Dashboard provides an overview of patient smoking habits, health conditions, and important health indicators using Microsoft Power BI.

The analysis helps identify patterns in smoking status across different age groups and genders. It also highlights the distribution of recorded health conditions across different organ categories and smoking exposure levels.

By comparing average cholesterol levels across blood pressure risk categories and analyzing cigarette consumption by smoking status, the dashboard helps management understand variations in patient health indicators.

Overall, the project transforms patient-level data into meaningful visual insights, supporting healthcare data exploration, patient monitoring, and informed management decisions.

## Final Conclusion

The Smoking and Health Analysis Dashboard was successfully developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze patient smoking habits, health conditions, and important health indicators.

Through this project, we explored the distribution of smokers across different age groups and genders, analyzed organ-wise health conditions, and compared cholesterol levels across different blood pressure risk categories. We also examined cigarette consumption patterns and the distribution of recorded health conditions across estimated smoking exposure levels.

The interactive dashboard provides a clear and organized view of patient data through KPIs, charts, and filters. It helps management identify important patterns, compare patient groups, and understand variations in health indicators.

Overall, this project demonstrates how data analytics and visualization can transform raw healthcare data into meaningful information, supporting better monitoring, exploratory analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

**Future Scope:** The dashboard can be enhanced by adding more patient records, historical health data, advanced analytics, and predictive models to explore healthcare trends in greater detail.

*This analysis is educational and exploratory. It does not establish medical diagnoses or prove that smoking causes specific health conditions.*
